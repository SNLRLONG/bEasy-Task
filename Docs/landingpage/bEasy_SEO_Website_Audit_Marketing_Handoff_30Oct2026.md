## 2. Homepage Audit

The homepage title is now strong, but the visible copy is still inconsistent with current operations and service availability.

### Recommended H1

Professional Home & Office Cleaning Services in Phnom Penh

Supporting copy: Book trained bEasy cleaners for hourly cleaning, deep cleaning, office cleaning, aircon cleaning, upholstery cleaning, washing machine cleaning and post-renovation cleaning.

### P0 homepage corrections

- Remove all references to Pest Control and discontinued General Cleaning from homepage body copy, FAQ, footer and any structured data.

- Replace “Easily reschedule or adjust your booking anytime” with policy-safe wording. Suggested: “Manage eligible bookings easily through bEasy. Rescheduling availability depends on the service type, booking status and applicable booking policy.”

- Replace “Rate and choose our top-rated professionals” with assignment-safe wording. Suggested: “Your service is assigned to trained and qualified bEasy cleaners or service professionals based on job requirements and availability.”

- Verify homepage social-proof metrics such as 2,000+ Happy Customers, 600+ Services Completed and 4.9 Average Rating. Every number should have a current source.

- Use one central service data source so inactive services disappear consistently from homepage, footer, FAQ and other shared modules.

## 3. URL & Indexing Hygiene

## 4. Service Page Audit & Required Changes

### Instant Cleaning

Good foundation; retain structure, update wording.

- Remove legacy comparison to General Cleaning; compare Instant Cleaning with Deep Cleaning instead.

- Avoid promising unrestricted extra hours. Use: “Additional cleaning time is subject to service rules, availability and the current booking.”

- Describe Instant Cleaning as flexible hourly / short-notice cleaning subject to available slots.

- Add related guides: House Cleaning Cost, Instant vs Deep, 1 Hour vs 2 Hours (future).

### Deep Cleaning

Needs substantial content expansion and correction.

- Correct obvious copy errors such as “vaetted” and “thrash”.

- Remove absolute wording such as “every area”. Use safe-access language.

- Update the page to the latest scope: bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchen, common areas, detailed grime/dust removal and applicable glass cleaning.

- Add approved add-ons: Ironing $5 up to 30 minutes; Wash & Change Bedsheet $5; House with Pets $3.

- Add exclusions: chandeliers, curtain/upholstery washing, floor polishing, heavy furniture moving, belongings-filled cabinets and unsafe access.

- Add related guides: How Often Should You Deep Clean?, Instant vs Deep, Move-In Checklist.

### Office Cleaning

P0 rewrite required; current page contains residential/deep-cleaning copy.

- Remove references to mattresses, sofas, carpets, AC vents/refrigerator interiors and house-size FAQ wording.

- Replace residential price cards with “Custom quotation”.

- Use scope aligned to offices: workstations, meeting rooms, reception, pantry, toilets, common areas, floors, bins, accessible glass and high-touch surfaces.

- Mention one-time and recurring office cleaning and monthly fixed invoicing for recurring agreements.

- Recommended H1: “Professional Office Cleaning in Phnom Penh”.

### Aircon Cleaning

Good commercial page but still contains copied Pest Control wording.

- Remove any wording such as “results that keep pests away”.

- Use benefit language around airflow, cooling performance, unit cleanliness and indoor comfort.

- Keep promotions (e.g. gas inspection/top-up) separate from permanent SEO copy unless the offer is intended to be permanent.

- Add related article: How Often Should You Clean Your AC in Cambodia?

### Washing Machine Cleaning

P0 scope conflict with current non-invasive SOP.

- Remove any promise of full dismantling as a standard cleaning method.

- Use: “non-invasive cleaning of accessible areas and safely removable parts where appropriate”.

- Soften unsupported absolutes such as “all major brands” and quantified odour-removal percentages.

- Add safety-stop language for faulty, unsafe, leaking or damaged units.

- Avoid recommending professional monthly cleaning unless Operations explicitly confirms it.

### Upholstery Cleaning

P0 corrections required for materials and guarantees.

- Remove or qualify leather cleaning. Current policy excludes unsafe/delicate materials such as leather, suede, silk and velvet unless separately approved.

- Remove absolute guarantee that upholstery cannot shrink or be damaged.

- Use pre-check wording: supported materials are inspected before cleaning; unsuitable/damaged materials may be declined.

- Remove “heat treatment” claims unless this is a standardized, verified bEasy process.

- Update sofa/mattress articles to reflect the same supported-material rules.

### Post-Renovation Cleaning

Strong content base, but several claims need tightening.

- Replace “remove all construction debris” with limited/scope-based wording; large/heavy construction waste should be separate.

- Remove “95–100% dust removal” percentage claims.

- Replace universal “4–8 hours / 2–6 cleaners” claims with size/scope-dependent manpower and duration.

- Remove Pest Control as an optional add-on.

- Keep clear distinction from Deep Cleaning and route renovation dust/paint/cement residue to this service.

## 8. Developer Technical SEO Checklist

### Redirects

- All permanent migrations return HTTP 301.

- No duplicate legacy page remains indexable after migration.

### Canonical URLs

- Every indexable page has one self-referencing canonical to the final URL.

- No canonical points to old /service-detail/ pages.

### XML Sitemap

- Returns HTTP 200.

- Includes only canonical/indexable URLs.

- Includes new articles.

- Excludes retired General/Pest pages and old duplicate service-detail URLs.

- Updates automatically on publish.

### robots.txt

- Returns HTTP 200.

- Does not block Googlebot from public content.

- References sitemap.

- Blocks admin/private routes where appropriate.

### Hreflang

- Reciprocal EN/KH links for equivalent pages.

- Add x-default if appropriate.

- Do not point article translations to unrelated homepage URLs.

### Structured Data

- Homepage: Organization/LocalBusiness.

- Service pages: Service.

- Articles: Article with headline, datePublished, dateModified, publisher and image.

- Navigation: BreadcrumbList.

### Images

- Use descriptive filenames.

- Use accurate alt text describing the actual image.

- Avoid keyword stuffing.

## 9. Backdated Article Handling

- Backdating is not itself an SEO advantage. The value comes from creating a coherent topical library quickly.

- Use datePublished for the editorial publication date being shown and dateModified for meaningful later edits.

- Do not repeatedly change dates purely to make content appear older or fresher.

## 10. Search Console & Measurement

The next stage is no longer “publish more because Google cannot find us”. The site now has enough SEO content to begin optimizing based on actual search performance.

- Track impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, query, country and device per page.

- Prioritize queries/pages ranking in positions 8-20; improving an existing page from position 14 to page one is usually more valuable than publishing another overlapping article.

- Connect organic landing pages to the bEasy customer journey: Service Viewed → Booking Started → Booking Details Completed → Slot Selected → Checkout Started → Purchase Completed.

- Ultimately report SEO by attributed bookings and revenue, not only traffic.
